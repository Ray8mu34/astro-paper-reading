# English guide

Use astro-paper-reading for graduate-level astronomy explanations. First orient a reader who has not opened the paper: problem and importance, actual difficulty, approach/contribution, results and scientific scope, and the argument role of each original numbered section. Then explore a small selection of consequential mechanisms in dependency order, with as much derivation as understanding needs.

Keep one scientific analysis.json, but separate audience prose from work records. reader.overview/roadmap/focus and public sections produce the article; claims, provenance, coverage, implementation and reproduction records produce analysis-notes.md. A conclusion-changing prior or calibration inconsistency belongs in science discussion; statements about what the agent did belong in records. This is sentence-function judgment, not a banned-word filter.

Prefer decisive original figures. Add interaction only when an explicit counterfactual increases understanding of the central argument. Record include/omit rationale internally. Figure counts, word counts and tool coverage are not success metrics. Captions state the scientific object and necessary simplifications briefly; solver details and tests remain available in the technical layer.

Load reading-method.md, reader-experience.md and scientific-integrity.md, then relevant type, statistics, domain and visualization references. Schema1.1 supports reader planning, audience separation and declarative embedded figures. The builder renders prepared scientific writing; it does not read papers. Legacy1.0 states and validated microlensing modules remain supported.
